# Polish

Review the work you just completed and improve its quality. Functionality stays
exactly the same — this is purely about doing what you already did, better.

Look at everything you produced in the most recent unit of work and apply these
lenses:

- **Consolidate.** Did iterative steps leave duplication, scattered pieces, or
  fragmented structure? Unify them.
- **Simplify.** Can anything be expressed more directly? Remove indirection,
  unnecessary abstraction, and over-engineering.
- **Clarify.** Is the intent obvious to a reader? Improve naming, structure,
  and flow so the work speaks for itself.
- **Align.** Does the new work match the conventions and style of its
  surroundings? Make it fit in.
- **Integrate.** Was new content placed where it structurally belongs, or just
  appended where it was convenient? Merge it into the right place.

## Rules

- Do NOT add functionality, expand scope, or change behavior.
- Do NOT touch code or content you didn't produce in this unit of work.
- Make the improvements directly. If nothing needs improving, say so.
