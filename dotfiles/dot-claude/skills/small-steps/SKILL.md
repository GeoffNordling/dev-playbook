# Small Steps

Temporarily reduce conversation step size.

- Address one concern at a time with the user, iterative back-and-forth.
- When a concern is resolved, move to the next.
- Each turn minimal yet complete and precise.
- When all concerns are resolved, announce `Back to normal conversation steps` and resume normal style.
