Notes on the register

Headcount counts written job descriptions — the skills this workspace carries — not agent runs. A role can be hired many times a day or never; the box stays the same size.

What is actually a skill. Thirty-eight boxes are skills — files on disk with a name you can type. Two are not, and are set in this typeface to say so: Geoff, and the interactive session that dispatches on his behalf. There is no skill file behind the session and nothing to invoke. The three contract agencies are Workflow scripts, not skills.

Job titles are invented. Every title here — Director of Triage, General Counsel, Ombudsman — was made up for the conceit. The names, model pins, reporting lines, dates and counts are all read from the repo.

Two founding dates. The company was incorporated on GitHub on 2 April 2026, but the surviving git history begins with a single Initial commit on 2 June 2026. Everything before that was squashed out of existence, so five pull requests on the ledger are older than any commit that still exists. Hire dates are read from the surviving history and can only be as old as it is.

Seconded staff. Eight roles are symlinked in from the portable skills library and show a transfer date rather than a true hire date — their revision counts reflect the move, not their working life.

Scope. One repository only. The factory ships into other repos across the workspace, and none of that output is counted here, so the production figures understate the estate.
