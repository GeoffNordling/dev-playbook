# Workstream Residual Ledger

Workstream's residual record: what
[headings and stints](/doc-types/workstream/contract-shape.md) cannot
express. That is all an entry is — a record.

## Workstreams

Per Workstream written to the encoding: what the headings and the
stints could not express, recognized and written down at the moment of
writing. Entries name headings and stints in the vocabulary
[encoding.md](/doc-types/workstream/encoding.md) declares.

An entry is a couple of sentences, hard limit: name each specific thing
the file could not express and why the shape cannot say it, nothing
else. A Workstream with nothing to record has no entry. No Workstream
is written yet, so the ledger holds none.
